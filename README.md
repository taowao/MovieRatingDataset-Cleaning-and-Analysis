# MovieRatingDataset-Cleaning-and-Analysis
Using MySQL to clean the dataset and using Excel to do Exploratory Data Analysis


## Questions for Exploratory Data Analysis

*1. General Dataset Overview*
How many movies are in the dataset?

What are the types of missing data (e.g., genre, duration, country)?

Which columns have the most missing or inconsistent data?

*2. Genre Analysis*
What are the most common genres across the dataset?

Which genres are associated with the highest average gross revenue?

How often do movies have multiple genres? (e.g., "Drama, Crime")

*3. Revenue and Budget Insights*
What is the distribution of box office revenue?

What are the top 10 highest grossing films?

Which directors have the highest average grossing movies?

Are certain genres associated with higher box office performance?

*4. Country and Language Trends*
What countries produce the most movies in this dataset?

Is there a correlation between country and genre popularity?

Are certain countries associated with higher box office revenue?

*5. Movie Duration and Ratings*
What is the distribution of movie runtimes? Are there outliers (e.g., "Inf", "NaN", or negative values)?

How do ratings (like "R", "PG-13") correlate with box office performance?

Do longer movies tend to perform better or worse financially?

*6. Director and Actor Insights*
Which directors appear most frequently?

Which directors consistently create high-rated or high-grossing films?

Are certain directors associated with specific genres?

*7. Release Trends*
Which years had the highest number of movie releases?

Are there temporal trends in genre popularity or revenue?

What months or seasons see the most releases or highest gross?

*8. Outliers & Anomalies*
Are there any extreme outliers in revenue, runtime, or year of release?

Identify examples of poor data quality (e.g., malformed dates, unusual characters, wrong data types).

*9. Cleaning-Driven Insights*
You could create a “Before and After Cleaning” section:

How many rows were fixed, filled, or dropped?

What were the biggest improvements after cleaning the data?

How did cleaning improve data usability for visualization or modeling?
